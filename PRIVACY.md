# Privacy Policy — Widgets: Lock Screen Calendar

**Effective Date:** November 28, 2026

**Widgets: Lock Screen Calendar** ("the App") is designed with a strict **Local-First & Privacy-First** architecture.

## 1. Data Storage & Processing
- **On-Device Storage:** Your schedules, tasks, countdowns, vocabulary progress, and custom Lock Screen wallpaper configurations are stored locally on your iPhone inside your private App Group container. We do not operate external tracking servers or sell user data.
- **Optional iCloud Sync:** When enabled in signed App Store builds, Apple CloudKit is used solely to synchronize your own data across your devices or within a private shared space you explicitly invite a partner or friend to join.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **Location (When In Use — Optional):** Used strictly at coarse accuracy (~1 km) to fetch real-time local weather (via Apple WeatherKit or Open-Meteo) for your Lock Screen widget and wallpaper. Coordinates are never linked to your identity.
- **Calendars & Reminders (Optional):** Used strictly on-device via Apple EventKit to display your upcoming events and incomplete reminders on your Lock Screen. Your calendar and reminder items never leave your device.
- **Photos (Add Only / Picker — Optional):** Used only when you choose a personal background photo or save a generated Lock Screen wallpaper to your Photo Library.

## 3. In-App Purchases
- All subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed securely and directly by **Apple App Store (StoreKit 2)**. We never have access to your credit card or billing details.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding privacy or support, please open an issue on our official support page: `https://github.com/nhavoquang/widgets-ios/issues`.
